# Employee Benefits & Perks at Nebula

## Health insurance

All employees are covered by the corporate voluntary health insurance (VHI, ДМС) program. The insurance starts on the first working day and includes outpatient care, hospitalization and emergency dental care. Full dental care coverage (check-ups, treatment, hygiene) becomes available after the probation period, that is after 3 months of employment. Employees can add family members to their insurance plan at a 50% discount.

## Sports and wellbeing

The company compensates sports expenses up to 24,000 RUB per year (2,000 RUB per month): gym memberships, swimming pools, yoga studios and sports apps all qualify. To get the compensation, upload the receipt to the HR portal before the 25th day of the month. Mental health support includes free sessions with corporate psychologists — up to 8 sessions per year, fully confidential.

## Learning and development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 50,000 RUB. It can be spent on professional courses, conferences, books and certifications. Corporate English classes are fully covered by the company and are not deducted from this budget; groups are formed twice a year by level.

## Stock options

Employees become eligible for the stock option program (программа опционов) after one full year of employment. Options vest over 4 years with a one-year cliff: the first 25% vests after the first year in the program, the rest vests monthly in equal parts. The strike price is fixed at the grant date valuation.

## Additional days off

Besides the main vacation policy, the company grants 3 extra paid days off per year as "wellness days" — no medical certificate required, they can be taken on short notice through the HR portal.
